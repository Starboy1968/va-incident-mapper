* Federal citation not independently re-checked by this tool.

Caveats

  • § 18.2-152.7:1 turns on the content (obscene, vulgar, lewd, or threats of illegal acts) plus intent to coerce, intimidate or harass. Rude but non-obscene messages may not qualify.
  • Va. Code § 18.2-60.3 requires conduct on more than one occasion and intent to cause fear of death, criminal sexual assault or bodily injury. Harassment alone does not satisfy it.
  • Linking alternate accounts to one person is an investigation question for law enforcement and platforms. Record observations; do not assert identity.
  • Creating an alternate account is not itself a crime. It is best used as evidence of intent and course of conduct in a stalking or harassment case.
  • Treating a platform ban as a CFAA violation is contested: after Van Buren v. United States (2021), breaking a terms-of-service rule generally does not by itself 'exceed authorized access'. Treat § 1030 here as unsettled and ask counsel.
