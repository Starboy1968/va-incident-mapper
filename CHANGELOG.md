# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-07

- Incident Logger: corrected legal labels (§ 18.2-60 is threats, stalking is § 18.2-60.3, doxxing is § 18.2-186.4, § 249 needs bodily injury, § 1028 and § 1985(3) narrowed, CFAA marked contested). Reports no longer say an incident "constitutes" a violation.
- Docs: GitHub documentation set, screenshots, contributing and security files.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-05

- CLI: 14 attack types, 25 statutes (Virginia checked 2026-10-05), message analysis, trend and compare, tamper-evident log, markdown and HTML reports, `import` for intake files.
- Guided intake form: plain-language wizard, feelings field, evidence fingerprinting in the browser, three report formats, .html and .txt downloads, email option.
- Incident Logger: rebuilt from the original dashboard (Log Incident, History, Export, Settings), session-only with Save and Open case file, .txt, .xlsx and .pdf exports, offline bundled libraries.
- Privacy: no storage, no network, Content-Security-Policy, contact details excluded by default.
