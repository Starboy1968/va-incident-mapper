# Code of conduct

This project serves people who may be in distress. Be respectful, patient and precise.

- Do not post real case details, names or evidence. Do not use this project to accuse or expose individuals.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and say when you are unsure.

Maintainers may remove content or block contributors who break these rules. Report concerns through a private security advisory on the repository.
